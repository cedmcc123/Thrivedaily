Privacy Policy

  

Last updated: May 1, 2026 · Applies to the Thrive Daily app and website

  

"Grow a little better, every day."

  

Effective date: May 1, 2026 · Applies to all Thrive Daily products

  
    

Your privacy matters deeply to us. Thrive Daily is built on the principle that your personal wellness data belongs to you — not us. This policy explains clearly what we collect, why, and how you can control it.

  
  

1. Who We Are

  

Thrive Daily ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Thrive Daily mental wellness check-in app and website at thriveDaily.app. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at hello@thrivedaily.app.

  

2. Information We Collect

  

We collect only what's necessary to provide a quality experience:

  
        
  • Check-in responses: Your answers to wellness questions are processed and stored locally on your device. We do not transmit individual responses to our servers.
    •     
  • Payment information: Payments are processed securely by Stripe. We never see or store your full credit card details. Stripe's privacy policy governs payment data.
    •     
  • Email address: If you sign up for reminders or contact us, we collect your email to communicate with you.
    •     
  • Usage data: We may collect anonymous, aggregated data (e.g. which screens are visited). This data cannot identify you personally.
    •     
  • Device & browser info: Basic technical information to ensure the app works correctly on your device.
    •   
  

3. How We Use Your Information

  

We use the information we collect to:

  
        
  • Provide and improve the Thrive Daily app and services
    •     
  • Process your subscription payments via Stripe
    •     
  • Send important account and service communications
    •     
  • Respond to your questions and support requests
    •     
  • Understand how our app is used so we can improve it
    •   
  

We do not use your data for advertising. We do not sell your personal information to any third party — ever.

  

4. Your Right to Cancel & Opt Out

  
    

🔓 How to cancel your subscription

    

You can cancel your Thrive Daily Premium subscription at any time using any of these methods:

    
          
  • In the app: Go to More → Manage / Cancel Subscription
    •       
  • Online: Visit the Stripe Customer Portal →
    •       
  • By email: Email hello@thrivedaily.app and we'll cancel for you within 24 hours
    •     
    

After cancellation, your Premium access continues until the end of your current billing period. We do not offer refunds for partial periods, but we will always try to do right by you — just email us.

    

🗑️ How to delete all your data

    

To permanently delete all your data from your device:

    
          
  • In the app: Go to More → Manage / Cancel Subscription → Delete all my data
    •       
  • By email: Email hello@thrivedaily.app — we'll confirm deletion within 30 days
    •     
    

Deleting your data is permanent and cannot be undone.

  
  

5. Data Storage & Security

  

Your check-in responses, journal entries, and garden data are stored locally in your browser's localStorage on your device. This data is not transmitted to our servers. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect any data we do hold against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

  

Payment data is handled exclusively by Stripe, which is PCI-DSS compliant. Review Stripe's Privacy Policy for details on payment data handling.

  

6. Cookies

  

Thrive Daily may use essential cookies to maintain your session. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some app functionality may be affected.

  

7. Third-Party Services

  

We use the following third-party services:

  
        
  • Stripe — payment processing (stripe.com/privacy)
    •     
  • Anthropic (Claude AI) — AI-powered insights and affirmations. Your mood data is sent to Claude's API to generate personalized responses. Anthropic's privacy policy applies (anthropic.com/privacy)
    •     
  • Google Fonts — typography loaded from Google's servers
    •

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

    •     
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
    •     
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
